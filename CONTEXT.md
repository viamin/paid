# Paid

Paid coordinates software work from intent gathering through delivery and
operational response. These terms describe its automation authority.

## Language

**Automation phase**:
A stage of responsibility for software work: discovery, issue preparation,
coding, merge, release & deployment, or operations. A phase describes the work regardless of whether
a person or automation starts it.

**Project automation default**:
Standing permission for Paid to perform configured automatic work in an enabled
phase for a project.

**Continuation authorization**:
An explicit, revocable grant to continue particular work automatically through
a chosen delivery endpoint, including phases disabled by project defaults.

**Work item**:
The scoped unit of requested work whose identity continues across its linked
issues, agent runs, and pull requests.

**Responsible phase**:
The phase that owns resolving a request, distinct from the phase where the work
originally arose.

## Outcome feedback vocabulary

These terms describe the proposal in RDR-082; they do not declare it delivered.

**Assessment**:
A recorded judgment by Paid about intended behavior, implementation, or
verification, scoped to the evidence and version it examined.

**Outcome feedback**:
A person's positive or negative judgment about experienced behavior, with
optional explanatory details.

**Human approval of behavior**:
Explicit positive feedback about the selected behavior and version. It is
distinct from permission to execute, merge, or release work.

**Default good**:
Provisional acceptance for ordinary continuation when no human feedback
exists. It supplies no human confirmation or evidence of correctness.

**Assessment attribution**:
A supported connection between a reported outcome and an assessment that
missed or misjudged it. The connection is separate from the person's feedback.

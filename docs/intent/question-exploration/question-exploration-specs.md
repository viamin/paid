# EARS Specs: Question Exploration

Status: the chat-entry and final-answer submission claims below are implemented;
diagram and durable-progress work remains planned.

- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-001** — When a user opens an enabled Inbox clarifying question, Paid SHALL reuse that user's most recently updated active conversation, or resume their most recently updated closed conversation for the Inbox item. Closed inline conversations SHALL support continued messaging, and closed conversations SHALL support pending tool confirmations, after an idle timeout, preserving their transcript and context. Closed workspace transcripts SHALL remain read-only until their workspace is restored through the explicit workspace-reopen control. Resuming an older conversation SHALL NOT alter any other conversation or its workspace. Archived conversations SHALL remain read-only and SHALL be replaced on Inbox open. Concurrent opens SHALL converge on the same conversation. Confirmation claiming and resumption SHALL atomically check current session state; rejected or duplicate confirmations SHALL NOT reopen a session. The legacy clarifying-question entry SHALL instead open one canonical linked chat, restoring an archived chat and retaining its linked issue/PR and pending-question context. The linked chat title SHALL identify its repository and issue or PR number, and reuse SHALL update an older title to that format.

  *Code:* `app/services/inbox/open_interactive_chat.rb`, `app/services/clarifying_questions/open_chat.rb`, `app/policies/chat_message_policy.rb`, `app/services/chat_sessions/resolve_tool_call.rb`, `app/services/chat_sessions/build_system_prompt.rb`, `app/views/chat_sessions/_conversation.html.erb`.
- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-002** — When the user confirms final ordered answers in a linked exploration chat, Paid SHALL post them through the standard clarifying-answer comment path, adjust labels, and remove the inbox item only after the comment succeeds. Users without issue-comment permission SHALL not be offered or able to invoke this action.
  *Tests:* `spec/mcp/tools/submit_clarifying_answers_spec.rb`, `spec/requests/projects/clarifying_questions_spec.rb`.

- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-003** — When Paid presents a diagram in chat, it SHALL identify the question, provide a text equivalent, allow expansion/collapse and keyboard interaction, and include a text input for comments on that diagram.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-004** — When a user comments on a diagram or selected element, Paid SHALL attribute the comment to the user and retain question identity and sufficient textual context for the comment to remain understandable after the diagram is removed.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-005** — When a diagram is replaced or discarded, Paid SHALL preserve human comments, answers and evidence summaries without requiring retention of non-final diagram source or visual revision history.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-006** — When Paid receives a comment against an outdated diagram element, it SHALL preserve the identified context or request clarification and SHALL NOT silently associate the comment with a different element.
- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-007** — While an authorized collaborator uses a shared exploration conversation, Paid SHALL enforce project and tenant access on transcript, message, diagram, subscription and tool operations and SHALL attribute actions to that collaborator rather than the session creator.

- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-008** — When selecting a question's exploration method, Paid SHALL investigate available facts first, adapt its aid to context, and ask how the person wants to investigate when the method is unclear.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-009** — When Paid renders a generated diagram, it SHALL validate its typed description, escape untrusted content and prevent generated scripts from executing in the application origin; invalid output SHALL leave a usable textual conversation.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-010** — When Paid initiates an investigation, it SHALL enforce the configured budget and execution policy, request authorization before exceeding either, and expose progress and cancellation for long-running work.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-011** — When a user selects, collapses or experimentally edits a diagram, Paid SHALL treat the interaction as exploration and SHALL NOT infer a preference or increase intent certainty solely from that interaction.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-012** — When an exploration action is available through the UI, Paid SHALL expose an equivalent authorized API/tool action and consistent message context through HTML, Cable and SSE transports.
- [ ] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-013** — When a conversation is archived or an investigation is interrupted, Paid SHALL preserve the related human answers and evidence; reopening SHALL restore current question progress without requiring discarded diagrams.
- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-014** — The interactive Inbox chat SHALL persist its Inbox item key, creator, opened/closed timestamps and queue-metadata audit snapshot. Only a user with the project’s comment authority SHALL create or use it. Inbox context SHALL be available through an explicit section-query service and SHALL NOT be inserted into the system prompt by default.

- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-015** — When an authorized user opens a legacy clarifying-question chat from an Inbox detail frame, Paid SHALL render only the linked chat panel inside that requested frame, preserving conversation delivery, message submission, older-message pagination, and the pane's reachable composer. Full-page destinations rendered inside the embedded panel — project links and the archive / unarchive controls — SHALL opt out of the requested frame (`data-turbo-frame="_top"`) so they navigate the full page rather than requesting a response with no matching frame or replacing the pane with an unrelated session. The full `/chat` route SHALL continue to render its normal session-list shell, and popup requests SHALL continue to render their popup variant.

- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-016** — When a linked exploration chat answers a strict choice question (ISSUE-ENHANCEMENT-018 markers), the answer validator SHALL accept every selection spelling that unambiguously picks an offered option: the canonical `Label (text)` line or the rendered `Label) text` echo (either optionally marker-prefixed and with appended rationale), the bare option label, or the option's 1-based number or letter. The chat context SHALL render choice questions with their canonical option lines and the accepted answer forms. A selection that picks no offered option SHALL still be rejected, and single/multi selection counting SHALL be unchanged.

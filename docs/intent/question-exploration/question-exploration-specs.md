Status: all claims below are implemented, including bounded investigation
(`QUESTION-EXPLORATION-010`) and its transport-parity slice

- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-001** — When a user opens an enabled Inbox clarifying question, Paid SHALL reuse that user's most recently updated active conversation, or resume their most recently updated closed conversation for the Inbox item. Closed inline conversations SHALL support continued messaging, and closed conversations SHALL support pending tool confirmations, after an idle timeout, preserving their transcript and context. Closed workspace transcripts SHALL remain read-only until their workspace is restored through the explicit workspace-reopen control. Resuming an older conversation SHALL NOT alter any other conversation or its workspace. Archived conversations SHALL remain read-only and SHALL be replaced on Inbox open. Concurrent opens SHALL converge on the same conversation. Confirmation claiming and resumption SHALL atomically check current session state; rejected or duplicate confirmations SHALL NOT reopen a session. The legacy clarifying-question entry SHALL instead open one canonical linked chat, restoring an archived chat and retaining its linked issue/PR and pending-question context. The linked chat title SHALL identify its repository and issue or PR number, and reuse SHALL update an older title to that format.

- [x] **QUESTION-EXPLORATION-010** — When Paid initiates an investigation, it SHALL enforce the configured budget and execution policy, request authorization before exceeding either, and expose progress and cancellation for long-running work. A run interrupted by worker death SHALL settle as failed and release its budget reservation rather than strand it. Experiment investigations SHALL reuse an already-ready isolated workspace and SHALL NOT provision a duplicate container while a provision is in flight. An experiment SHALL execute its prompt through the question's isolated chat-workspace runtime within a bounded wall clock, retain the bounded output, exit status and truncation evidence on the investigation for reconnecting collaborators, and SHALL NOT report workspace readiness alone as experimental evidence; a non-zero exit or runtime failure SHALL settle as failed with the partial output preserved. Research investigations SHALL label their result with the knowledge-search scope actually executed and SHALL report explicitly when no evidence matched. A cancellation landing before or during execution SHALL win over settlement and SHALL NOT consume the reserved budget.
